# Thirty days to a more usable Dutch vocabulary

## Target

**Introduce 1,000 high-use Dutch vocabulary entries, then confirm as many as possible through accurate recall on at least two separate days.** This is a stretch goal, not a guarantee of fluency, and the app's score is not an independent estimate of total vocabulary size.

If you already know many of the first 1,000 items, use **Quick familiarity check** to label up to 40 known items at a time without using your daily new-word allowance, or mark individual cards "I already know" as you go. Verify those items in later reviews. These familiar items count as *introduced*, not *verified*, until you can type them unaided. Add a missing word immediately when a real conversation exposes a gap.

## Daily protocol

1. **Review due words first**. Type the Dutch answer *before* showing it. Treat `de`/`het` as part of the word for nouns.
2. **Introduce up to 35 new entries per day.** For an easy familiar word, use "I already know"; it returns for confirmation. For unfamiliar items, guess, reveal if necessary, and do the review again later.
3. **Recall words actively**, in English → Dutch direction. If the answer is revealed or mistyped, use "Again" rather than giving yourself credit.
4. **Log 1–3 real missing words** you encountered speaking, reading, or thinking that day. The goal is to close real communication gaps, not merely hit a number.
5. **Back up progress** once a week (Settings → Download progress backup).

Suggested routine: two short sessions per day, one in the morning and one later, with a quick look at difficult words before sleep. Aim for consistency; reduce the daily number if reviews become overwhelming.

## Week-by-week focus

| Phase | Days | Main emphasis | Meaningful outcome |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Start | 1–7 | Connectors, pronouns, prepositions, highest-use verbs | Easier to start and connect everyday thoughts |
| Build | 8–14 | More verbs, time, frequency, location and opinion words | More ways to express what happened, happens, or will happen |
| Expand | 15–21 | Descriptions, feelings, numbers and people | More precise requests, opinions and explanations |
| Apply | 22–30 | Home, shopping, travel, work and personalized gaps | Better coverage of conversations you actually need |

A daily goal of 35 introduces about 1,000 entries over 29 days, **but not all will be verified by day 30** because two-day recall requires time and repeated review. Keep reviewing after the month to consolidate the bank. Do not count every new entry as already learned.

## Weekly checkpoint

- **Day 7:** Which words do you need most often but still cannot retrieve? Add them.
- **Day 14:** Inspect *Due* and *Learning*; consider lowering new words if the review queue is overloaded.
- **Day 21:** Practice producing real one-sentence utterances using the newly available verbs and connectors.
- **Day 30:** Compare `Introduced`, `Verified recall`, and `Due`. The **Verified** metric is the most conservative one. Plan a 2-week maintenance period for words still due.

## What success looks like

- **Quantity:** 1,000 core entries encountered at least once (unless you deliberately choose a smaller load).
- **Accuracy:** most studied items can be typed from English prompts on separate days.
- **Transfer:** fewer real-world moments when you cannot find a Dutch verb, connector, modifier, or everyday noun.
- **Honest measurement:** recognize that typed-recall results are more meaningful than simply looking at the translation.
